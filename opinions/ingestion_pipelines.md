# Opinion: Automated Ingestion & Capture Pipelines

## 1. Description
The biggest failure point in a second brain is the effort required to put data *into* it. This opinion mandates automated "pipelines" that capture information from your digital life (YouTube, Browser, University Portal) with zero manual effort.

## 2. Technical Implementation
* **Browser Extension:** (e.g., Nexus-style) to clip articles and summarize videos instantly.
* **File Watcher:** A background service monitoring `~/Documents/University` and `~/Downloads` for new PDFs.
* **Metadata Extraction:** Using small local models (like Llama-3-8B) to tag and summarize data at the point of entry.

## 3. Benefits
* **Zero-Effort Growth:** Your digital brain grows automatically as you browse and study.
* **Searchability:** High-quality metadata makes retrieval 50% more accurate.
